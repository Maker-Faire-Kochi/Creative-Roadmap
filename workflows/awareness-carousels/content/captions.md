## 10 · What makes Maker Faire different from a competition?

The core invitation is to show, share and learn. Individual Faires may include contests or awards, but competition does not define the whole experience. A useful conversation can begin with a work in progress and an honest account of what it taught you.

Maker Faire Kochi · Coming soon.

## 11 · What is the Maker Movement?

The Maker Movement is the wider culture of learning by doing, experimenting and sharing knowledge. A Faire is one place that culture becomes visible. The next experiment can happen long after everyone goes home.

## 12 · What is the history of Maker Faire?

Make: magazine launched in 2005. The first Maker Faire followed in the San Francisco Bay Area in 2006, bringing the people and projects behind that culture into a shared physical space. One gathering became the start of a much wider story.
