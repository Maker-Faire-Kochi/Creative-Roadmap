## Illustration

Use straight, slotted members with plausible connections. Opaque faces and quiet side planes establish volume; restrained fine lines describe depth and joins. Preserve the broad beam silhouette, plain pins and mechanical clarity of the [restored baseline](../references/illustration/straight-beam-baseline.png).

Solve one illustration before extending the language across many applications. Check the silhouette at thumbnail size as well as detail at the intended export size. A mechanism should be understandable through its geometry; labels support that understanding.

Start with neutral structure. Use color to draw attention to an action, distinguish parts or states, or improve contrast. Do not recolor every part merely to display the full palette. The mechanism samples use red for drive input and cyan for the selected assembly. The restored Open study has its own neutral/blue palette, retained in [beam-palette.json](../references/illustration/beam-palette.json).

Keep conceptual machines clearly described as illustrations. An attractive assembly or a passing geometry audit does not establish a fabrication-ready engineering design.

## Make Some Noise campaign

Lead with a clear graphic idea: the word NOISE deforms as a controlled signal, visibly related to a physical maker object. Use one dominant image event and a deliberate reading order. Support dense, energetic lettering with calm space. Avoid decorative filler and unrelated effects. The original speaker is a generated conceptual object; the camera/slider adaptation is original editable geometry built with Anatomy.

The media-team poster must make recruitment immediately apparent. Keep **JOIN THE MEDIA TEAM** strong enough to read after the headline. Preserve all five roles: **Videographers, Video Editors, Photographers, Writers and Designers**. Eligibility is **open to volunteers across Kerala**. The invitation is **Help tell the makers’ stories.**

The recorded signup destination is `https://makerfaire.in/volunteer`; it was checked in the original work on 13 October 2026 and has not been reverified for this repository packaging. Before a new campaign release, check the destination and the rendered QR. Keep a clear signup instruction, a readable plain URL and the full QR quiet zone.

A 9:16 story should reflow the hierarchy and account for interface overlays. Preserve proportions and readability when moving from a 4:5 post. The reference exports are digital artwork, not calibrated print separations.

## Local specificity and evidence

Prefer a making action that reads immediately at final scale. Beypore Uru construction, coir rope-making and cheenavala mechanisms are documented local reference subjects. Preserve creator names, original links and licenses. Stock filming/soldering photos must not be presented as documented Kerala participants. Generated objects must not be presented as photographed exhibits.

Do not invent event dates, venues, deadlines, experience requirements or performance claims. Distinguish selected creative direction, a design hypothesis, a technical check and measured audience evidence.

## Production practice

Keep runtime inputs, fonts, palettes and assets inside this project when implementation resumes. Do not import or symlink to Design System. Preserve prior iterations for substantial changes, inspect actual rendered compositions, and verify specialized behavior before reporting it complete. Keep Figma and Paper unchanged until explicitly requested.
