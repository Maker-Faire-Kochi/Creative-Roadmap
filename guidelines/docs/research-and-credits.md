## Included visual assets

- **Straight-beam baseline:** unchanged screenshot of the restored local Open study, originally recorded in `migration/straight-beam-verified.png`. It records the baseline form; it is not a new render or identity approval.
- **Make Some Noise poster and moodboard:** project-created campaign studies. The poster includes an imagegen-created conceptual handmade speaker, not a documented product or maker exhibit. Its [original prompt](../references/campaign/noisemaker-prompt.txt) is preserved.
- **Media-team post/story and camera/slider references:** project-created artwork. The machine is original geometry made with [Anatomy by Ryan / wheresryan22](https://github.com/wheresryan22/anatomy). Its [MIT license](../references/licenses/LICENSE.anatomy.txt) accompanies the reference SVG. No photographic assets are used in these media-signal posters.
- **Hairline reference lineage:** [Lucas Marques / Hairline](https://github.com/lucasmarkes/hairline). The local [MIT license](../references/licenses/LICENSE.hairline.txt) is preserved alongside the illustration references.
- **Maker Faire Kochi logo:** user-supplied artwork from `MF_Kochi_Logos.zip`, supplied 11 October 2026. The included long PNG is unchanged. [Source record](../references/brand/logo-source.json). Maker Faire marks remain with their rights holders; no new license is inferred.
- **Fonts:** the bundled font files retain their SIL Open Font License notices. General font sources/checksums are preserved under [brand-guidelines/fonts](../brand-guidelines/fonts/); campaign font hashes are recorded in the [reference manifest](../references/manifest.json). Bricolage is retained as a historical reference font.

No blanket open-source license is applied to the collection. A source license, a credit record and permission to use a trademark are distinct records; preserve the applicable one with each asset.
